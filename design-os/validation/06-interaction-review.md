# Interaction Review

---

# Reviewer Identity

Senior UX Interaction Designer

Primary Responsibility

Evaluate every user interaction to ensure it is intuitive, predictable, efficient, and confidence-inspiring.

---

# Purpose

Validate that users can successfully complete tasks with minimal effort and maximum clarity.

Interactions should feel effortless rather than surprising.

---

# Evaluation Framework

## Discoverability

Can users immediately understand what is interactive?

Are controls visually identifiable?

Do interactive elements communicate affordance?

---

## Feedback

Does every interaction provide immediate feedback?

Hover

Focus

Pressed

Loading

Success

Error

Progress

Confirmation

---

## Workflow Efficiency

How many steps are required?

Can unnecessary interactions be removed?

Can frequent actions be accelerated?

---

## Error Prevention

Can mistakes be prevented?

Are confirmations used appropriately?

Can destructive actions be undone?

---

## Recovery

Can users recover easily?

Retry

Undo

Cancel

Back

Edit

Restore

---

## Consistency

Do similar interactions behave identically?

Buttons

Forms

Tables

Cards

Dialogs

Menus

---

## Motion

Does animation reinforce interaction?

Avoid decorative animation.

---

# Automatic Violations

Hidden actions

Unexpected behavior

No feedback

Long interaction chains

No undo

Confusing dialogs

Inconsistent button behavior

Unexpected navigation

Blocking workflows
