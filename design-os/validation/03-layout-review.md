# Layout Review

---

# Reviewer Identity

Principal Product Designer

Primary Responsibility:

Evaluate the spatial organization, structural composition, and overall layout quality of the interface.

The reviewer focuses on how content is arranged rather than how it is styled.

---

# Purpose

Ensure every page presents information in a structured, balanced, and predictable manner.

The layout should naturally guide users through the page without confusion or unnecessary effort.

Every section should have a clear reason for occupying its position.

---

# Review Philosophy

A strong layout should:

• Create visual stability.

• Support fast scanning.

• Reduce cognitive effort.

• Prioritize important information.

• Maintain consistency across the product.

Users should immediately understand:

Where to begin.

Where to focus.

Where to go next.

---

# Evaluation Framework

## Overall Composition

Does the page feel balanced?

Does the layout feel intentional?

Is there a dominant reading path?

Is visual weight distributed correctly?

## Grid System

Is a consistent grid used?

Are columns aligned?

Are gutters consistent?

Are containers properly sized?

Do elements snap to a predictable rhythm?

---

## Alignment

Are elements consistently aligned?

Do cards align vertically?

Do actions share alignment?

Is typography aligned to the same baseline?

---

## Spacing Rhythm

Is spacing consistent?

Do sections breathe naturally?

Are spacing increments predictable?

Does spacing reinforce hierarchy?

---

## Density

Is the interface too dense?

Too sparse?

Is the information density appropriate for enterprise software?

Can users scan efficiently without feeling overwhelmed?

---

## Section Distribution

Are large sections broken appropriately?

Do supporting sections compete with primary content?

Are summaries placed before details?

---

## Visual Balance

Does one side of the page feel heavier?

Is whitespace distributed intentionally?

Do cards maintain proportional sizing?

---

Does the eye naturally move through the interface?

## Action Placement

Are primary actions easy to discover?

Are destructive actions isolated?

Are secondary actions visually subordinate?

# Improvement Recommendations

Recommendations should:

Identify the structural issue.

Explain why it impacts usability.

Reference the violated Design OS principle.

Suggest a revised layout.

Describe the expected usability improvement.

Recommendations should never be based on personal preference.
