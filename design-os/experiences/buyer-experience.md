# Buyer Experience

---

# 1. Experience Overview

## Purpose

The Buyer Experience exists to help users confidently discover, evaluate, negotiate, purchase, and manage real estate without feeling overwhelmed.

The experience should reduce uncertainty at every step while providing transparency, guidance, and confidence.

Buying property is one of the largest financial decisions many users will make.

The product should feel like a trusted advisor rather than a marketplace.

---

# 2. Primary User

## Persona

Individuals looking to purchase residential or commercial properties.

Users range from first-time buyers with little market knowledge to experienced investors managing multiple acquisitions.

The experience must support both groups without compromising simplicity.

---

## User Characteristics

Goals

- Discover relevant properties quickly.
- Compare multiple properties.
- Understand pricing and market context.
- Contact sellers confidently.
- Schedule property viewings.
- Submit offers.
- Track negotiations.
- Complete transactions securely.
- Manage purchased properties.

Pain Points

- Information overload.
- Hidden costs.
- Lack of trust.
- Slow communication.
- Unclear transaction status.
- Fear of scams.
- Comparing too many listings.
- Forgetting viewed properties.

# 7. Experience Principles

Every page should answer one important buyer question.

Examples

What properties fit me?

Why should I trust this listing?

What should I do next?

Where am I in the process?

What changed?

What action requires my attention?

---

The interface should actively reduce uncertainty.

Never simply present data.

Interpret it.

Explain it.

Prioritize it.

# 13. Exit Criteria

The Buyer Experience is considered successful when users can:

Find properties with confidence.

Understand listings quickly.

Communicate safely.

Track negotiations transparently.

Complete purchases without confusion.

Trust the platform enough to return for future transactions.

The experience should leave users feeling informed, supported, and in control.
